Description complète

Human Resources Workload: 50% - 100% Place of work: Level of experience: With work experience FlexOffice: on-site Shape people, leadership and organization through transformation Group IT is at the heart of our transformation. Technology, new capabilities, changing operating models and evolving ways of working are reshaping how we operate as an organization. We are looking for an experienced Senior HR Business Partner who knows how to navigate this kind of complexity and turn business transformation into successful people and organizational outcomes. In this role, you will partner closely with senior leaders across Group IT and act as a trusted advisor, challenger and transformation partner. You will bring a strong business perspective, sound HR judgement and extensive experience in guiding leaders and organizations through significant change. This is a role for someone who is comfortable operating at senior level, navigating ambiguity and moving between strategy and implementation.

What you'll be doing

  • Partner with senior Group IT leaders as a trusted advisor and challenger, translating business priorities into a clear and forward-looking people agenda.
  • Shape and accompany major transformation initiatives, including organizational redesign, evolving operating models, restructuring and changes to roles, skills and capabilities.
  • Advise leaders on the people and organizational implications of technology and business transformation, bringing clarity and pragmatic solutions to complex situations.
  • Drive strategic workforce planning, anticipating future capabilities and addressing questions around reskilling, talent, sourcing and organizational capacity.
  • Coach and challenge senior leaders and leadership teams through transformation, ambiguity and significant organizational change.
  • Connect business needs with HR expertise across Talent, Learning & Development, Compensation and Recruiting to deliver integrated, business-focused solutions.
  • Use people and organizational insights to strengthen leadership, talent, succession and organizational effectiveness while ensuring effective delivery of key HR processes.

What you'll bring to the role

  • You bring extensive experience as a senior HR Business Partner in a large, international organization, ideally in insurance or financial services or in a technical environment.
  • You bring a strong academic foundation, with a degree from a university or university of applied sciences.
  • You have successfully guided large-scale business, organizational or technology transformations, turning complex change into meaningful outcomes.
  • You partner confidently with senior executives and leadership teams, building the credibility to advise, challenge and influence decisions.
  • You combine expertise in organizational design, change management and strategic workforce planning to help shape future-ready organizations.
  • You understand how IT and digital transformation reshape capabilities, sourcing models and ways of working-and what this means for people and reskilling.
  • You think strategically and bring strong business acumen, connecting business priorities with technology, organizational needs and people.
  • You build strong stakeholder relationships through clear communication, sound judgement and a resilient, solution-oriented approach. You are comfortable working in German and English
  • French, Spanish or Italian is a plus.
