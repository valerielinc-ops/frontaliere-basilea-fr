Asset Management Workload: 50-100% Place of work: Level of experience: With work experience FlexOffice: on-site Set new standards and shaping the future together. With us, you don't just tick tasks off a list. You work alongside people who take responsibility, set high standards and have the courage to try new approaches. We believe trust and performance go hand in hand. Your ideas, your initiative and your contributions are what really matter to us. You will have the opportunity to make a difference for our customers, your team and your own personal development. Shape investment decisions that matter. Join our Asset Allocation & Regional Investment Management team and help translate global investment strategies into portfolio decisions across international insurance businesses. This role offers a unique opportunity to build expertise in multi-asset investing, portfolio management and insurance asset allocation within a dynamic environment.

What you'll be doing

  • Support tactical asset allocation, portfolio rebalancing and overlay implementation for insurance portfolios.
  • Contribute to the implementation of investment strategies across different market units.
  • Prepare investment committee materials, analyses and decision papers.
  • Collaborate with investment, finance and risk teams to optimize portfolio risk-return profiles.
  • Give Market Units access to Group expertise, systems and standard processes to reduce duplication and implementation costs
  • Contribute to the ongoing development of investment processes, methodologies and governance frameworks.
  • Assist with reporting, ad-hoc analyses and strategic projects.

What you'll bring to the role

  • Master's degree in Finance, Economics, Mathematics or a related field.
  • First experience in asset management, investments, insurance or financial markets.
  • Strong interest in multi-asset investing and asset allocation.
  • Analytical mindset with strong quantitative and problem-solving skills.
  • Affinity for data analytics, programming and AI-supported tools is an advantage.
  • Structured, reliable and proactive team player with a strong sense of ownership.
  • Ability to communicate effectively with diverse stakeholders.
  • Fluent in German and English.

What We Offer

We offer broad exposure to institutional asset management and insurance investing, close collaboration with experienced investment professionals, and the opportunity to contribute to asset-allocation decisions in an international environment. You will gain valuable insights into portfolio construction, risk management and insurance investment strategy while further developing your analytical and quantitative skills and growing your career within a global investment organization.
